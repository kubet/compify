Compify's current original source is licensed under **GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0 only** (`AGPL-3.0-only`), except where a nearer file or directory
notice identifies different terms.

The AGPL is a strong network-copyleft license. If an operator modifies the
covered program and users interact with that modified version over a network,
section 13 requires the operator to offer those users the Corresponding Source.
Self-hosters must point the source offer at their own deployed modifications,
not only at upstream Compify.

A separately signed commercial license may be available for original Compify
rights controlled by the applicable copyright holder. Contact
[hello@compify.app](mailto:hello@compify.app). Third-party obligations are not
removed by a commercial agreement.

Earlier Compify repository states validly received under MIT remain under that
prior grant. The recorded final MIT boundary is commit
`b0d0945c9b406c201d80528616715b699822ad03`; history and immutable tags are not
rewritten to imply revocation.
