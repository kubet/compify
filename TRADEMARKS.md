Compify project. The software license grants copyright permissions; it does not
grant a trademark license beyond reasonable, factual references to the project.

You may:

- state accurately that software is based on or compatible with Compify;
- link to the official project; and
- retain legally required attribution and provenance notices.

Without prior written permission, you may not:

- present a fork, hosted service, package, or company as the official Compify
  project;
- use Compify branding in a way that suggests sponsorship or endorsement; or
- use a confusingly similar product name, logo, domain, or package identity.

Forks should use their own name and visual identity while preserving required
copyright and license notices. Questions or permission requests may be sent to
**hello@compify.app**.

This policy does not claim that every mark is registered and does not limit uses
that applicable trademark law permits without consent.
